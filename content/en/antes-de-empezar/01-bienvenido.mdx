## Welcome to Atlas IA

You are about to start a journey through the world of Artificial Intelligence.

It does not matter whether you are a teacher, a professional, a student or simply someone who is curious. **Atlas IA** has been created so that anyone can understand AI without needing to be an engineer or a programmer.

## What is Atlas IA?

Atlas IA is an **educational platform** designed to teach you Artificial Intelligence from scratch up to an advanced level.

But it is not just another course.

It is a complete guide that combines:

- **Clear concepts** explained in plain language
- **Real examples** you can apply today
- **Comparisons** between tools so you can make your own decisions
- **Hands-on activities** to learn by doing
- **A community** of people who, like you, want to understand AI

## What is it for?

So that you can:

1. **Understand what AI is** and what it is not
2. **Use AI tools with judgment**, not just because they are trendy
3. **Make informed decisions** about when and how to use AI
4. **Stay up to date** in a field that changes constantly
5. **Teach better** if you are a teacher, or **work better** if you are a professional

## What you will find

The platform is organized into **thematic blocks**:

| Block | Topic | What you will learn |

| 1 | Fundamentals | What AI, ML, DL, LLM are... |

## Your first step

You do not need to install anything. You do not need to pay anything. You do not need prior experience.

You only need **curiosity** and **the desire to learn**.

<Callout type="tip">
Start at the beginning and move at your own pace. Each lesson is designed to build on the previous one. Do not skip steps — the foundation is the most important thing.
