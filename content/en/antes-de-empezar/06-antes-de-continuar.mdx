## Before You Continue

You are about to enter **Block 1: AI Fundamentals**, which is where the real technical learning begins.

Before taking that step, make sure everything is clear.

## What you need

### Technical requirements

- **An up-to-date web browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- **An internet connection**
- **A free account** on at least one AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini)
- **The desire to learn** (this is no joke, it is the most important requirement)

### What you do NOT need

- You do not need to know how to program
- You do not need knowledge of mathematics
- You do not need a powerful computer
- You do not need to pay for anything
- You do not need to speak Spanish (everything is available in English)

## The right mindset

### Be curious

Curiosity is your compass. If something is not clear to you, **ask**. If something surprises you, **investigate**. If something seems odd, **verify**.

### Be patient

You will not understand everything the first time. And that is fine. Some concepts need to be reread, practised and reviewed several times before they "click".

### Be sceptical

Do not believe anything just because an AI told you. Do not believe anything just because you read it here. **Think for yourself.** Question everything. That is the basis of critical thinking.

### Be practical

Knowledge without practice is useless. Every concept you learn, **try it out**. Every tool you discover, **use it**. Theory without practice is just entertainment.

## What comes now

**Block 1: AI Fundamentals** is the most important one on the whole platform.

Here you will learn:

- What Artificial Intelligence really is (and what it is not)
- Machine Learning and Deep Learning explained without formulas
- What Large Language Models (LLMs) are and why they matter
- Transformers: the architecture that changed everything
- Tokens, context and inference: the pieces of the puzzle
- Hallucinations, biases and limitations: what AI cannot do

These concepts are the **foundation of everything else**. Without them, the rest of the blocks will be confusing.

<Callout type="info">
Spend as much time on Block 1 as you need. There is no rush. It is better to take two weeks to understand it well than to finish it in two days without having absorbed it.
</Callout>

## Tips for the road

1. **Do one lesson a day.** No more. Consistency beats occasional effort.

2. **Practise every day, even if it is 15 minutes.** Open an AI and experiment. You do not need a plan; just use curiosity as your guide.

3. **Do not compare yourself to others.** Your pace is your pace. Someone who has been using AI for months may seem "more advanced", but they may have huge conceptual gaps.

4. **Document your journey.** Create a document where you write down what you learn. Your own words will be your best notes.

5. **Share what you learn.** Explain it to a colleague, a friend or a family member. If you can teach it, you understand it.

## One last piece of advice

AI is not going to disappear. It is not a passing fad. It is going to be here for decades, transforming every industry, every profession, every way of working.

Learning AI now is not just an investment in your career. It is an investment in your **ability to adapt** to the world that is coming.

And the most important thing: **this is only the beginning**.
