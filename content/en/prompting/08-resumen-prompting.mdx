## What you have learned

In this block you have covered the complete journey of prompt engineering:

### The fundamentals
- **What a prompt is**: The instruction you give to the AI
- **Why it matters**: The quality of your input determines the quality of the output
- **What prompt engineering is**: The art of designing effective instructions

### The anatomy of a good prompt
1. **Role** - Who the AI is
2. **Context** - Relevant information
3. **Task** - What you want exactly
4. **Format** - How you want the response
5. **Constraints** - Limits and conditions
6. **Examples** - Samples of the expected result

### The techniques
| Technique | What it is for |
|---------|---------------|
| Zero-shot | Simple tasks without examples |
| Few-shot | Tasks with a defined pattern |
| Role | Specialised perspective |
| Chain-of-Thought | Step-by-step reasoning |
| Tree-of-Thought | Exploring multiple solutions |
| ReAct | Reasoning + searching for information |

### Adaptation by model
- **ChatGPT**: Direct, versatile, all-rounder
- **Claude**: Deep, nuanced, analytical
- **Gemini**: Factual, concise, with sources

## The 10 golden rules

1. **Be specific**: A specific prompt gives a specific response
2. **Provide context**: The AI does not know your situation unless you tell it
3. **Define the format**: Tables, lists, paragraphs: say it explicitly
4. **Iterate**: Your first prompt will not be the best. Refine, test, improve
5. **Use roles**: Assigning a role drastically improves quality
6. **Set constraints**: Limits on length, tone, audience
7. **Add examples**: Few-shot is surprisingly effective
8. **Ask step by step**: For complex problems, ask for reasoning
9. **Adapt to the model**: ChatGPT is not the same as Claude or Gemini
10. **Always verify**: AI makes mistakes. Confirm important data

## Common mistakes you should avoid

### ❌ The vague prompt
"Write something about AI"

### ❌ The prompt without context
"Translate this into English" (without specifying formal/informal)

### ❌ The prompt without constraints
"Give me ideas for my business" (without a limit on quantity or focus)

### ❌ The prompt that assumes knowledge
"Explain transformers" (without knowing whether the person asking knows deep learning)

### ❌ The prompt that does not iterate
Using the first result without asking for improvements

## Your daily checklist

Before sending an important prompt, review this list:

- [ ] Does the prompt have a clear objective?
- [ ] Have I provided enough context?
- [ ] Is the output format defined?
- [ ] Are the constraints appropriate?
- [ ] Have I considered which model to use?
- [ ] Am I willing to iterate if it is not perfect?

## The future of prompting

Prompt engineering is evolving rapidly:

### Current trends
- **Autonomous agents**: The AI takes multiple steps without intervention
- **Multimodal**: Prompts that combine text, images and audio
- **Personalisation**: Prompts that adapt to the person using them
- **Automation**: Prompts that generate other prompts

### Complementary skills
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: Combining prompts with search
- **Fine-tuning**: Training models with your data
- **Evaluation**: Measuring the quality of responses

<Callout type="tip">
Prompt engineering is not a passing fad. It is a fundamental skill for any professional who wants to make the most of AI. The better you communicate with AI, the more value you will get from it.
</Callout>
