## An effective prompt has structure

Just as a cooking recipe has ingredients and steps, a good prompt has key elements that make it work. You do not always need to use all of them, but knowing them will help you design better prompts.

## The 7 key elements

### 1. Role
Tell the AI **who it is**. This sets the tone, depth and focus of the response.

**Without role:** "Explain what machine learning is"
**With role:** "You are a university AI professor teaching first-year students. Explain what machine learning is"

### 2. Context
Give the AI **relevant information** about the situation.

**Without context:** "Write an email"
**With context:** "Write an email to a customer who has just bought our software. They want to know how to install it. The tone should be friendly and professional."

### 3. Specific task
Say exactly **what you want** it to do. Be concrete.

**Vague:** "Tell me about Python"
**Specific:** "Compare Python and JavaScript on 3 aspects: execution speed, ease of learning and use in web development"

### 4. Output format
Indicate **how you want** the response: list, table, paragraphs, code...

**Without format:** "Give me tips to save money"
**With format:** "Give me 5 tips to save money in table format: Tip | How to apply it | Estimated savings"

### 5. Constraints
Set **limits** on the response: length, tone, audience, terms to avoid...

**Example:** "Explain what a transformer is in under 100 words, as if you were explaining it to a 12-year-old."

### 6. Examples (few-shot)
Show the AI **examples** of the kind of response you expect.

**Without example:** "Translate to English: The cat is on the roof"
**With example:** "Translate these sentences into English following this format:
'The dog runs fast' → 'The dog runs fast'
'The cat is on the roof' → ..."

### 7. Step by step
Ask it to **reason** before answering (Chain-of-Thought).

**Normal:** "Calculate 24 × 37"
**Step by step:** "Calculate 24 × 37 step by step, explaining each operation"

## Universal template

```
[Role] You are an [expert in X] specialising in [Y].
[Context] I am currently [situation] and I need [need].
[Task] I want you to [specific description of the task].
[Format] Respond in [list/table/paragraphs] format.
[Constraints] Use a [formal/informal] tone, maximum [N] words, aimed at [audience].
```

### Practical example

```
You are a digital marketing expert specialising in LinkedIn.
I am currently creating my professional profile and I need to highlight my experience in AI.
I want you to write a profile headline and a 3-line summary.
Use a professional but accessible tone, aimed at tech recruiters.
```

## Common mistakes

### Asking for everything at once
**Bad:** "Make me a business plan, a market analysis and a financial forecast"
**Good:** Ask for one thing at a time or structure the prompt into sections

### Being too vague
**Bad:** "Help me with my work"
**Good:** "I need to prepare a 10-minute presentation on AI trends in 2026 for my sales team"

### Not specifying the format
**Bad:** "Tell me things about AI"
**Good:** "Give me 5 practical applications of AI in the healthcare sector, in table format with a benefit column and an example"
