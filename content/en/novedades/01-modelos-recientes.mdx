## The current LLM landscape

The language model ecosystem is evolving at a dizzying pace. Between 2024 and 2026 we have gone from context windows of 128K to models with more than one million tokens, and from high prices to a collapse in inference cost. This lesson offers an overview of the most relevant models in mid-2026.

<Callout type="info">
The pace of innovation in LLMs is so fast that any up-to-date list can become outdated within weeks. Consider this lesson as a snapshot of the state of the art in July 2026.
</Callout>

## GPT-5.6 (OpenAI)

Released in July 2026, the GPT-5.6 family is OpenAI's most recent generation. It comes in several variants aimed at different uses:

- **GPT-5.6 Sol:** the highest-capacity model, designed for complex agents and demanding professional work
- **GPT-5.6 Terra and Luna:** versions with a better balance between cost, speed and performance
- **Context of over 1M tokens:** processes complete books, huge codebases or very long conversations
- **Natively multimodal:** text, image, audio and video in a single model

OpenAI has already announced the development of the next generation, **GPT-6**.

## Claude 5 (Anthropic)

The Claude 5 generation arrived in 2026 with three distinct models:

### Claude Fable 5

- **Deep reasoning:** designed for difficult tasks and high-value autonomous work
- **1M token context**
- **Premium price:** it is Anthropic's most expensive paid model

### Claude Sonnet 5

- **Everyday driver:** excellent value for money
- **Ideal for coding, agents and computer use**
- **1M token context** at a competitive price

### Claude Opus 5

The most recent version (July 2026) impressed with its ability to **generate complete prototypes** — such as functional 3D video games with physics and music — from a single prompt.

<Callout type="tip">
If you work with autonomous agents, Claude Sonnet 5 and Fable 5 are two of the most highly regarded options in 2026 for their ability to use tools and sustain long chains of work.
</Callout>

## Gemini 3 (Google)

Google has consolidated its Gemini 3 line throughout 2025 and 2026:

- **Gemini 3.5 Flash:** fast multimodal model available since May 2026, ideal for high-volume tasks at low cost
- **Gemini 3.5 Pro:** launched in July 2026 after a complete redesign
- **Huge context:** the Gemini 3 Pro generation reached 2M tokens, a leader in processing long documents
- **Full multimodal:** video, audio, text and images in real time

## Grok 4.5 (xAI)

The company xAI has established itself as a serious frontier competitor:

- **Real-time access:** integration with X for up-to-date information
- **Strong at coding and tool use**
- **500K token context**
- **Competitive price:** among the lowest of the frontier models

## Open source: DeepSeek V4, Qwen, Llama 4 and Mistral

The open-source ecosystem has matured spectacularly:

### DeepSeek V4

The Chinese startup DeepSeek shook up the sector with unprecedented prices:

- **V4 Flash:** around $0.14 per million input tokens, a cost that has collapsed the market
- **V4 Pro:** high-quality reasoning and code at a fraction of the price of proprietary models

### Qwen 3.7 (Alibaba)

Alibaba's Qwen family is one of the most complete in the open ecosystem, with variants optimised for different sizes and tasks.

### Llama 4 (Meta)

Although it no longer sets the frontier, Llama 4 remains the most mature ecosystem for deploying your own models locally, with huge tooling support.

### Mistral Large 3 (Mistral AI)

The European startup continues to be Europe's benchmark, with multilingual models and deployment options respectful of data sovereignty.

<Callout type="info">
In 2026 the saying goes that "one tier below the frontier is open": open models such as DeepSeek V4, Qwen and Llama match proprietary ones on many tasks at a fraction of the cost.
</Callout>

## Key trends

1. **Giant context:** the million-token mark is becoming the standard
2. **Price collapse:** open-source competition has drastically reduced inference cost
3. **Agents by default:** models are designed to use tools and act, not just respond
4. **Multimodality as standard:** text, image, audio and video in a single model
5. **Specialisation:** models fine-tuned for specific domains (code, medicine, law)
