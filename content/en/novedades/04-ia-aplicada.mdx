## From theory to practice

AI advances do not stay in research laboratories. Increasingly, they translate into concrete applications that transform entire industries. This lesson explores the fields where AI is having the most significant impact in 2026.

<Callout type="info">
The distance between a research advance and its practical application has shrunk dramatically. What is a scientific paper today is a product tomorrow and part of our daily lives the day after.
</Callout>

## Science and research

### Drug discovery

- **AlphaFold 3:** predicts protein structures with high accuracy
- **Generative AI for molecules:** designs new drugs from scratch
- **Autonomous laboratories:** robotic systems that design, run and analyse experiments without constant supervision
- **Clinical trial simulation:** reduces development time and cost

### The recognition of AI in science

In 2024, the Nobel Prize in Chemistry recognised Demis Hassabis and John Jumper for AlphaFold, and the one in Physics went to Geoffrey Hinton and John Hopfield for neural networks. AI stopped being a peripheral tool to become a protagonist of science.

### Climate change

- Climate models with unprecedented resolution
- Optimisation of renewable energies
- Monitoring of deforestation and emissions from satellites
- Design of materials for carbon capture

### Mathematics and physics

- **AlphaGeometry:** solves olympiad-level geometry problems
- **Theorem discovery:** AI that proposes and proves new theorems
- **Quantum simulation:** models that predict particle behaviour

## Health

### Assisted diagnosis

- Radiology: tumour detection with accuracy comparable to that of a specialist professional
- Dermatology: identification of skin lesions
- Ophthalmology: early detection of retinopathy
- Pathology: automated analysis of biopsies

### Personalised medicine

- Treatments adapted to genetic profile
- Prediction of drug response
- Continuous monitoring with wearable devices
- Personalised prevention plans

### Mental health

- AI-assisted therapy (with human supervision)
- Early detection of depression and anxiety
- 24/7 emotional support chatbots
- Analysis of language patterns for diagnosis

## Education

### Personalised tutoring at scale

Platforms that adapt content, pace and style to each student, something that until now was only possible with one-to-one human tutors. Tutor agents, capable of guiding learning without giving away the answer directly, are one of the most promising applications.

### Teacher support

Tools that automate the creation of materials, marking and feedback, freeing up time for personal interaction. This very course is an example: its content is designed to be updated continuously.

### Adaptive assessment

Exams that adjust in real time to each student's level, providing a more accurate measurement of their knowledge.

## Productivity and work

### Coding assistants

Cursor, Copilot and Claude Code have gone from curiosities to essential tools. In 2026 they no longer just complete code: **they run complete tasks**, from writing a function to deploying an application.

### Work agents ("digital employees")

Platforms that combine agents with access to corporate tools, memory and controlled autonomy are changing administrative work:

- Automated document processing
- Generation of reports and presentations
- Intelligent email and calendar management
- Data analysis and generation of valuable conclusions

### Augmented creativity

- Assisted graphic design (Canva, Adobe Firefly)
- Music composition (Suno, Udio)
- Video with native audio (Veo 3.1, Runway)
- Writing and text editing

| Sector | Current impact | Future potential |
|--------|----------------|------------------|
| Health | Assisted diagnosis | Full personalised medicine |
| Education | Adaptive tutoring | Assisted continuous learning |
| Science | Drug discovery | Autonomous research |
| Productivity | Work agents | AI executive assistants |
