## Multimodality as the norm

If 2024 was the year multimodality became mainstream, 2025 and 2026 have been the years in which it was perfected. The ability to process and generate multiple formats is no longer a differentiator: it is an expected standard.

<Callout type="info">
The current frontier of multimodality is not in processing multiple formats, but in doing so in real time, with coherence between modalities and with native generation (including sound).
</Callout>

## Native video

Video generation has taken a qualitative leap in 2026: clips of 20 to 60 seconds, character consistency between shots, native 1080p or 4K resolution and, above all, **synchronised audio generated in the same pass**.

### Veo 3.1 (Google)

- **Synchronised native audio:** generates dialogue, effects and ambience that match the image
- **Realistic physics:** convincing movements that obey plausible physical laws
- **High fidelity:** among the best visual quality on the market

### Sora 2 (OpenAI)

- **Prompt understanding:** follows complex instructions with narrative coherence
- **World simulation:** scenes with internal physical logic
- **Worth noting:** OpenAI retired Sora's consumer app in 2026 and focused the model on its API

### Kling 3.0 (Kuaishou)

- **Best value for money** for creators
- **Lip-sync and multi-shot narration** for social content
- Wide and affordable access

### Runway Gen-4.5

- **Editing workflow:** the built-in editor remains the best in the sector
- **Brand consistency:** ideal for marketing and creative production
- **Fine control of camera and movement**

### Other options

**Seedance 2.0** (ByteDance) stands out in multi-image and multi-video narration, and **Wan 2.6** is the most efficient open option for video generation.

## Audio and real-time voice

### Native voice-to-voice

Frontier models (GPT-6, Gemini 3.8) process audio directly, without intermediate transcription:

- **Tone and emotion detection:** they respond to mood
- **Natural interruption:** you can interrupt as in a human conversation
- **Multiple voices:** selection of voice personality
- **Minimal latency:** response in milliseconds

### ElevenLabs Conversational AI

- Autonomous voice agents for customer service
- Real-time voice cloning
- Language detection and simultaneous translation
- Integration with telephony systems

<Callout type="tip">
AI voice assistants have matured: in 2026, interacting by voice with an AI system is as natural as doing so with another person.
</Callout>

## Unified models

The trend towards single models that process all modalities:

- **GPT-6 (Astra):** a single model for text, image, audio and video
- **Gemini 3.8:** natively processes text, image, audio and video
- **Claude 5.1:** vision and audio integrated with reasoning
- **DeepSeek V4 and Qwen:** open multimodal at low cost

### Advantages of unified models

- **Coherence:** a single line of reasoning underlies all modalities
- **Efficiency:** a single deployment instead of multiple models
- **Cross-modal reasoning:** relationships between modalities that separate models do not capture

## Emerging applications

### Real-time video analysis
- Smart surveillance with contextual understanding
- Automated sports analysis
- Live content moderation

### Multimodal translation
- Voice-to-voice translation with tone preservation
- Automatic subtitling with perfect synchronisation
- Image description for accessibility

### Unified content creation
- From a text prompt to a complete video with music and narration
- Multimodal editing: change the text and the video adjusts
- Generation of interactive prototypes (games, apps) from descriptions
