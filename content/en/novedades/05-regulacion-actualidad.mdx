## A world in regulation

While technology advances, governments and international bodies are racing to establish regulatory frameworks. August 2026 is a milestone: most of the obligations of the European AI Act enter full application.

<Callout type="info">
AI regulation is one of the most complex political challenges of our era. Every regulatory decision balances innovation, fundamental rights, competitiveness and security.
</Callout>

## AI Act: from approval to application

The European Union's AI Act, approved in 2024, is already in full application phase:

### Implementation timeline

- **1 August 2024:** entry into force of the regulation
- **2 February 2025:** prohibitions on unacceptable risk and AI literacy obligations
- **2 August 2025:** obligations for general-purpose AI models (GPAI)
- **2 August 2026:** obligations for high-risk systems (Annex III)
- **2027-2028:** high-risk systems integrated into regulated products (Annex I)

### State of application in 2026

- The **European AI Office** coordinates the application, which started on 2 August 2026
- **Transparency guidelines** published (labelling of synthetic content and deepfakes)
- Mandatory registration in the **European database of high-risk systems**
- Fines of up to **35 million euros or 7% of global turnover** in the most serious cases

### The Digital Omnibus debate

The European Commission proposed a simplification package in November 2025 ("Digital Omnibus") that could postpone part of the high-risk obligations until December 2027. Legislative uncertainty is a challenge for companies that must decide when to comply.

### European push for technological sovereignty

- **AI Gigafactories:** call to build AI supercomputers with more than 30 billion euros of investment
- **Project EUROPA:** a European consortium will build a frontier open-source model in the EU's 24 languages

## Regulation in other regions

### United States

- Executive orders on AI safety
- Mostly state-level regulation (such as California's SB 1047)
- Voluntary accountability framework
- Federal investment in safe research

### China

- Strict regulation of generated content
- Mandatory watermarks on all synthetic content
- Control of recommendation algorithms
- Government approval for public models

### Latin America

- **Brazil:** comprehensive bill inspired by the AI Act
- **Mexico:** national AI strategy with an emphasis on inclusion
- **Chile:** AI policy with a focus on human rights
- **Colombia:** roadmap for responsible AI

<Callout type="tip">
If you work with AI in Latin America, pay special attention to regulatory developments in Brazil and Mexico, which are setting the pace in the region.
</Callout>

## International treaties

### Framework Convention on AI (Council of Europe)

First binding international treaty on AI. Signed by more than 50 countries, it **entered into force in 2025** and establishes principles of:

- Human rights and democracy
- Rule of law
- Transparency and oversight
- Responsibility and reparation

### Beijing Declaration

Agreement between Global South countries for inclusive and equitable AI, with an emphasis on closing the digital divide.

## Emerging ethical debates

### Copyright and training

Multiple lawsuits by creators against AI companies continue to set precedents. The balance between fair use and compensation remains unresolved.

### Deepfakes and disinformation

The ability to generate realistic synthetic content has led to:

- Specific legislation against deepfakes (including the European one)
- Mandatory watermarking systems
- Synthetic content detection tools
- Verification protocols for the media

### Labour impact

AI automation is transforming the labour market faster than expected:

- New emerging professional profiles (agent orchestration, AI governance)
- Job retraining programmes
- Debate on universal basic income
- Unions of digital workers

<Callout type="warning">
The labour impact of AI is not in the future: it is already happening. Continuous training is no longer optional.
</Callout>

## Towards responsible AI

### Voluntary initiatives

- Frontier Model Forum: safety commitments from the major companies
- Agentic AI Foundation: open governance of agent protocols under the Linux Foundation
- AI Safety Summits: international coordination meetings

### Pending challenges

- **Global coordination:** how to regulate technology that does not understand borders
- **Regulatory speed:** how to legislate something that changes every month
- **Enforcement:** how to verify that companies comply
- **Global equity:** preventing regulation from benefiting only rich countries
