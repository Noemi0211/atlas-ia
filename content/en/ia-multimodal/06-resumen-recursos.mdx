## Block summary

In this block we have explored the world of multimodal AI, where models process and generate information in multiple formats.

### Lesson 1 — Introduction
Multimodal AI integrates text, image, audio and video into a single system. Models such as GPT-4o, Gemini and Claude lead this capability through architectures that fuse different modalities.

### Lesson 2 — Image and vision
DALL-E, Midjourney and Stable Diffusion allow generating images from text. Visual prompt engineering is key to getting good results. AI-assisted editing simplifies tasks such as removing backgrounds or restoring photos.

### Lesson 3 — Video and animation
Runway, Pika and Sora are revolutionizing video generation. AI-assisted editing (Descript, CapCut) speeds up the process. Virtual avatars and dynamic presentations expand the educational possibilities.

### Lesson 4 — Audio and voice
ElevenLabs and OpenAI TTS offer hyperrealistic voice synthesis. Whisper transcribes audio with great accuracy. Voice cloning raises opportunities and ethical risks. Suno and Udio generate music from prompts.

### Lesson 5 — Integrated applications
The real potential lies in combining modalities: complete educational videos, multimodal assistants, content localization and universal accessibility. Consistency between formats is key to a professional result.

## Block glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Multimodal** | Ability to process multiple formats (text, image, audio, video) |
| **Encoder** | Component that converts raw data into numerical representations |
| **Early fusion** | Combination of modalities before main processing |
| **Late fusion** | Separate processing and combination at the end |
| **Visual prompt** | Text description that guides the generation of an image |
| **Voice synthesis (TTS)** | Conversion of text into spoken audio |
| **Transcription (STT)** | Conversion of audio into text |
| **Diffusion** | Generation technique that reverses a noise process |
| **Deepfake** | Audiovisual content falsified with AI |
