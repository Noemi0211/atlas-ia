## The visual revolution

AI image generation has gone from being a curiosity to becoming a professional tool. Models such as DALL-E, Midjourney and Stable Diffusion make it possible to create photorealistic images from text descriptions.

<Callout type="info">
AI image generation is not only useful for creating art: it is a practical tool for designing educational materials, prototyping products, illustrating concepts and communicating ideas visually.
</Callout>

## Image generation

### How it works

Image generation models (diffusion) learn to reverse a noise process: they start from a random noisy image and refine it step by step until it matches the text description.

### Main tools

**DALL-E 3** (OpenAI)
Integrated with ChatGPT. Excellent for generating images from detailed descriptions. Handles text inside images well.

**Midjourney**
A benchmark for artistic quality. Ideal for stylized, conceptual and creative images. Used through Discord.

**Stable Diffusion**
Open source and free. Can be run locally. Great flexibility and an active community.

**Canva Magic Studio**
Integrated into Canva. Perfect for teachers and content creators who already use the platform.

| Tool | Quality | Ease of use | Price | Ideal for |
|------|---------|-------------|-------|-----------|
| DALL-E 3 | High | Very easy | Pay per use | General use |
| Midjourney | Very high | Medium | Subscription | Conceptual art |
| Stable Diffusion | High | Difficult | Free | Technical uses |
| Canva Magic | Medium | Very easy | Freemium | Educational materials |

## Visual prompt engineering

Creating good images with AI requires learning to describe them correctly:

### Elements of a good visual prompt

1. **Main subject:** what or who appears
2. **Action:** what it is doing
3. **Environment:** where the scene takes place
4. **Style:** realistic, watercolor, 3D, cartoon, etc.
5. **Lighting:** natural, dramatic, golden
6. **Composition:** close-up, wide shot, drone
7. **Color palette:** warm tones, monochrome, pastel

**Example:**

> "A robot teacher helping students in a classroom of the future, digital illustration style, warm lighting, soft colors, medium shot"

<Callout type="tip">
For best results, write the prompt in English. Image generation models are trained mainly with English descriptions and understand visual nuances better in that language.
</Callout>

## Image editing with AI

### Smart editing tools

- **Photoshop (Generative Fill):** fills, expands or removes objects with AI
- **Clipdrop:** removes backgrounds, relights and remixes images
- **Runway:** edits images with text instructions
- **Removal.ai:** removes backgrounds automatically

### Useful features for teachers

- Removing backgrounds from photographs
- Changing the visual style of an image
- Expanding images to change their format
- Restoring old or damaged photos

## Image analysis and understanding

Multimodal models can also **read** images:

- Extract text from documents (OCR)
- Describe charts and diagrams
- Identify objects, people and scenes
- Answer questions about visual content

### Educational applications

- Digitizing handwritten notes
- Creating alternative descriptions for students with visual impairments
- Analyzing works of art, maps or infographics
- Verifying consistency between text and image in teaching materials

## Current limitations

- **Visual hallucinations:** AI can add elements that were not requested
- **Copyright:** the legal status of generated images is still under debate
- **Bias:** models reflect biases from their training data
- **Consistency:** difficult to keep the same characters across images

<Callout type="warning">
Always check the copyright policy of the tool you use. Some platforms claim rights over the generated images, while others assign them to the user.
</Callout>
