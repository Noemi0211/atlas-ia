## Smart sound

Audio is perhaps the most accessible modality of multimodal AI. From voice assistants to music generation, AI audio tools have matured quickly and deliver surprising results.

<Callout type="info">
AI voice synthesis has reached a level of naturalness that makes it indistinguishable from a real human voice. This opens up incredible possibilities for education, accessibility and content creation.
</Callout>

## Voice synthesis (text to speech)

### Main tools

**ElevenLabs**
A benchmark in voice synthesis. Offers hyperrealistic voices with control over pitch, speed and emotion. Allows cloning voices with just a few minutes of audio.

**OpenAI TTS**
Integrated into the OpenAI API. High-quality voices with multiple options. Ideal for applications that already use the OpenAI ecosystem.

**Google Cloud TTS**
Wide selection of voices in multiple languages. Offers WaveNet, Google's most advanced synthesis technology.

**Microsoft Azure Speech**
Neural voices with customization. Ideal for business and educational applications.

### Educational uses

- Converting text to audio for students with visual impairments
- Creating educational audiobooks and podcasts
- Automatic narration of presentations
- Practicing pronunciation in languages

## Audio transcription (audio to text)

### Whisper (OpenAI)

OpenAI's transcription model is a benchmark in accuracy, supporting more than 90 languages. It can transcribe, translate and identify multiple speakers.

### Practical tools

- **Otter.ai:** real-time transcription for meetings and classes
- **Fireflies.ai:** automatic recording and transcription
- **Descript:** transcription plus audio and video editing

<Callout type="tip">
Whisper is free and open source. You can run it locally without an internet connection, which guarantees the privacy of the transcribed data.
</Callout>

## Voice cloning

Voice cloning makes it possible to replicate a person's voice with just a few minutes of sample audio. This has legitimate applications as well as significant risks.

### Ethical uses

- Preserving the voice of people with degenerative diseases
- Dubbing educational content into multiple languages
- Creating audiobooks narrated by the author themselves

### Risks

- Identity impersonation
- Disinformation through fake audio
- Phone scams using cloned voices

<Callout type="warning">
Voice cloning without consent is ethically questionable and is illegal in many countries. Always obtain explicit permission before cloning someone's voice.
</Callout>

## Music generation

### AI for music

**Suno AI**
Generates complete songs with lyrics, melody and instrumentation from a prompt. Supports multiple musical styles.

**Udio**
Similar to Suno, with an emphasis on production quality. Allows refining generated songs.

**AIVA**
An AI composer trained with classical music. Ideal for soundtracks and instrumental music.

**MusicGen (Meta)**
Open-source model for music generation. Can be run locally.

| Tool | Output type | Control | Quality |
|------|-------------|---------|---------|
| Suno AI | Complete songs | Low | High |
| Udio | Complete songs | Medium | Very high |
| AIVA | Instrumental music | High | High |
| MusicGen | Music fragments | Medium | Medium |

### Educational applications

- Creating songs to memorize concepts
- Generating background music for presentations
- Composing soundtracks for student projects
- Exploring music theory in a practical way

## Audio separation and enhancement

- **Moises.ai:** separates voices and instruments from any song
- **Adobe Podcast:** improves the quality of voice recordings
- **Krisp:** removes background noise in real time
