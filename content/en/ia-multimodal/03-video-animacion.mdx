## Video as a medium

Video is the most widely consumed content format today. AI is democratizing its creation, allowing anyone to produce quality videos without the need for expensive equipment or advanced technical knowledge.

<Callout type="info">
AI video generation is in its early stages compared to still images, but it is advancing at a dizzying pace. What seems like magic today will be standard tomorrow.
</Callout>

## Video generation with AI

### Main tools

**Runway Gen-3**
A leader in video generation. It makes it possible to create clips from text, images or existing video. Includes motion brush and camera control.

**Pika Labs**
Video generation with motion control. Ideal for short animations and social media content.

**Sora (OpenAI)**
Not yet available to the general public. Promises photorealistic videos of up to 60 seconds with an understanding of real-world physics.

**Kaiber**
Focused on music videos and artistic content. It allows transforming images into stylized animations.

| Tool | Maximum duration | Control | Quality |
|------|------------------|---------|---------|
| Runway Gen-3 | 18 sec | High | Very high |
| Pika Labs | 5 sec | Medium | High |
| Sora | 60 sec | High | Excellent |
| Kaiber | 10 sec | Low | High |

## AI-assisted video editing

### Features available today

- **Automatic cutting:** removes silences, pauses and repetitions
- **Transcription and subtitles:** generates automatic synchronized subtitles
- **Quality enhancement:** increases resolution, stabilizes, reduces noise
- **Translation and dubbing:** translates the audio and synchronizes lip movement
- **Smart search:** finds specific moments within the video

### Editing tools

- **Descript:** text-based video editor. You edit the transcript and the video adjusts automatically
- **CapCut:** free editor with powerful AI features (subtitles, effects, enhancement)
- **Premiere Pro (Adobe):** AI features for professional editing
- **DaVinci Resolve:** free AI tools for color grading and correction

<Callout type="tip">
Descript allows you to edit video as if it were a text document: delete words and the video cuts itself. It is one of the most revolutionary tools for content creators.
</Callout>

## Animation with AI

### From still image to motion

Current tools can animate static images:

- **Move AI:** animates characters from a photo
- **Animated Drawings (Meta):** brings children's drawings to life
- **Runway Motion Brush:** paints which parts of the image should move

### Virtual avatars

Creation of virtual presenters from text or audio:

- **HeyGen:** realistic avatar that reads your script
- **Synthesia:** creates videos with virtual presenters
- **D-ID:** animates photographs so they can speak

### Educational applications

- Creating explainer videos without having to record yourself
- Generating animations of abstract concepts
- Producing content in multiple languages with the same video
- Developing accessible materials with sign language

<Callout type="warning">
AI-generated avatars can be confusing for students if it is not clearly stated that they are not real people. Use them transparently.
</Callout>

## Dynamic presentations

Tools such as **Gamma** and **Tome** are transforming static presentations into interactive experiences:

- Adaptive non-linear navigation
- Embedding of AI-generated video and audio
- Automatic coherent design
- Real-time updates with external data

## Limitations and challenges

- **Short duration:** generated clips last only a few seconds
- **Inconsistency:** characters and scenes change between shots
- **Computational cost:** video generation requires a lot of computing power
- **Deepfakes:** risk of malicious use to create fake content
