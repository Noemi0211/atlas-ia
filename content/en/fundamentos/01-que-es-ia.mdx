## What is Artificial Intelligence

We have all heard about Artificial Intelligence. But if someone asked you right now "what exactly is it?", could you answer clearly?

Probably not. And it's not your fault. There is a lot of confusion around this term.

## The simple definition

Artificial Intelligence is a **field of computer science** that seeks to create systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

That includes:

- Understanding natural language
- Recognizing images
- Making decisions
- Learning from experience
- Generating content

The key is the word **"simulate"**. AI does not "think" the way we do. It has no consciousness or emotions. What it does is **process data** and find patterns to produce results that seem intelligent.

## The three levels of AI

Not all AI is the same. There are three levels you should distinguish:

### Narrow AI (ANI)

This is the AI that exists today. It is designed for **one specific task**. Siri, ChatGPT, your camera filter (all of that is narrow AI). It is very good at what it does, but it cannot do anything else.

It is like a specialist who only knows about one thing.

### General AI (AGI)

It would be an AI with cognitive capabilities **similar to a human's**. It could learn any task, reason, adapt and be creative.

**It does not exist.** Not yet. Some researchers believe it will arrive in a few years. Others think it is decades away.

### Superintelligence (ASI)

It would be an AI that **surpasses human intelligence** in every aspect. For now, it is the realm of science fiction.

<Callout type="info">
When you hear "AI is going to replace teachers", ask yourself: what level of AI are they talking about? Today's narrow AI can help, but it cannot replace the human capacity to understand, empathize and adapt.
</Callout>

## What AI is and what it is NOT

This distinction is fundamental:

### It IS AI

- A language model like ChatGPT or Claude
- A medical image recognizer
- A Netflix recommendation system
- An automatic translator
- A voice assistant

### It is NOT AI

- A program that runs predefined instructions
- A search algorithm in Google (although it uses AI to improve results)
- A script that automates repetitive tasks without learning
- An IF-THEN rule system
- A chatbot that only shows pre-recorded responses

<Callout type="warning">
Many products are sold as "AI" when in reality they are simple automation. If a system does not **learn from data** and does not **improve with experience**, it is probably not real AI.
</Callout>

## The three eras of AI

### Era 1: Rule-based systems (1950-1990)

AI followed rules written by humans. If A happens, do B. It worked for simple problems, but it did not scale.

### Era 2: Machine Learning (1990-2017)

Machines started to **learn from data** instead of following rules. This was a fundamental shift.

### Era 3: Deep Learning and LLM (2017-today)

Models became enormous, capable of learning incredibly complex patterns. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — all of this is Era 3.

## Why it matters to understand this

If you do not understand what AI really is, you will make bad decisions:

- You will invest in "AI" tools that are not
- You will expect things that AI cannot do
- You will neglect skills that AI cannot replace
- You will miss real opportunities to improve your work

<Callout type="tip">
AI is a powerful tool, but it only works well when you understand what it can and cannot do. That is exactly what we are going to build in this course: **judgment**.
</Callout>
