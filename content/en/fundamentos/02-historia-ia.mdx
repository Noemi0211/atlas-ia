## A brief history of AI

Artificial Intelligence was not born with ChatGPT. It has a history of **more than 70 years**, full of brilliant moments, inflated expectations, frustrations and rebirths.

Understanding this history will help you understand why AI is what it is today.

## The origins (1950-1960)

### Alan Turing and the Turing Test (1950)

It all started with a question: **Can a machine think?**

Alan Turing published a paper proposing a test: if a machine could fool a human into believing it was human, then it could be said to "think".

### The baptism: Dartmouth Conference (1956)

A group of researchers met in Dartmouth, United States. There they coined the term **"Artificial Intelligence"** and laid the foundations of the field.

Their optimism was enormous. They predicted that within 20 years machines would do everything a human does.

They were wrong. But not forever.

## The AI winter (1970-1990)

### First crisis (1974-1980)

Expectations were too high. Computers were too slow. Projects did not deliver what was promised. Funding evaporated.

This period is called **"the AI winter"**. The money disappeared, the researchers scattered.

### The rise of expert systems (1980-1987)

**Expert systems** revolutionized industry. They were programs that imitated the reasoning of human specialists in specific fields.

They worked well, but they were expensive to maintain and did not scale.

### Second winter (1987-1993)

Expert systems turned out to be fragile. When the market collapsed, AI went out of fashion again.

## The rebirth: Machine Learning (1993-2011)

### Neural networks return

The idea of neural networks had been dead for decades, but researchers like **Geoffrey Hinton** kept working on it.

In 2012, everything changed.

### ImageNet: the Sputnik moment of AI

A team used **deep neural networks** to recognize images. The result was so impressive that the research world turned towards Deep Learning.

The revolution had begun.

## The modern era (2012-today)

### AlphaGo defeats the world champion (2016)

DeepMind created an AI that defeated the best Go player in the world. Go was the last "human" game that AI had not conquered.

### Transformers: the paper that changed everything (2017)

Google published **"Attention Is All You Need"**. This paper introduced the **Transformer** architecture, which is the foundation of all current language models.

Without Transformers, ChatGPT would not exist.

### GPT-3 demonstrates the power (2020)

OpenAI showed that with enough data and parameters, a language model can do amazing things.

### ChatGPT changes the world (2022)

ChatGPT was launched in November 2022. In 2 months it reached 100 million users. It was the fastest-growing application in history.

### The war of the models (2023-today)

GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Mistral — competition has intensified like never before. Quality has gone up, prices have gone down and accessibility has increased.

<Callout type="info">
What we are experiencing now is unprecedented in the history of technology. No technology has ever grown so fast or been adopted so massively.
</Callout>

## What history teaches us

### The expectation cycles

AI follows a pattern:

1. **Excess of expectation** → Everyone believes AI will change everything
2. **Disillusionment** → Delivery does not meet expectations
3. **Realism** → It is understood what it can and cannot do
4. **Real adoption** → The tool is integrated productively

We are in the transition from point 1 to point 3. It is the best time to learn: neither in the media fever, nor in the pessimism of the winter.

### Speed accelerates

The first AI revolution took **20 years**. The second took **15 years**. The third has taken **5 years**. The fourth will take **2 or 3 years**.

Each cycle is faster than the previous one.

<Callout type="tip">
If you wait for AI to "stabilize" before learning, you will never start. AI is not going to stabilize. It is going to keep changing. The best strategy is to learn to navigate change, not to wait for it to stop.
</Callout>
