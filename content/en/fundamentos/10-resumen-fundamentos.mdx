## Summary and self-assessment

## What you have learned

### Lesson 1: What AI is
- AI is a field of computer science that seeks to simulate human intelligence
- There are three levels: narrow (current), general (future), superintelligence (theoretical)
- Not all automation is AI

### Lesson 2: History of AI
- AI has more than 70 years of history
- It has gone through cycles of euphoria and disillusionment
- Transformers (2017) changed everything
- We are in the era of LLMs

### Lesson 3: Machine Learning
- ML = learning from data instead of following rules
- Three types: supervised, unsupervised, reinforcement
- Key concepts: features, model, loss, generalization

### Lesson 4: Deep Learning
- Neural networks with many layers
- Each layer learns more complex abstractions
- CNN for images, RNN for sequences, Transformers for everything

### Lesson 5: LLM
- Models trained on billions of tokens of text
- They predict the most likely next word
- Emergent capabilities appear with size
- RLHF makes them useful and safe

### Lesson 6: Transformers
- They process all the text at once (not in sequence)
- Attention mechanism: understanding relationships between words
- They are the foundation of all modern models

### Lesson 7: Tokens, context and inference
- Token = the model's text unit (≈ 0.75 words in Spanish)
- Context window = how much the model can "see"
- Inference = using the trained model to generate responses

### Lesson 8: Hallucinations and limitations
- LLMs invent information with total confidence
- Biases come from the training data
- Always verify critical data with primary sources

### Lesson 9: Main models
- GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini are the big three
- Llama 3 is the best free option
- There is no universal "best" model
