## The main models

The language model ecosystem is huge and grows every week. Here I present the most important ones so you have a clear map of the landscape.

## The big three

### GPT-4o (OpenAI)

**The all-rounder par excellence.**

- Excellent for most tasks
- Integrated into ChatGPT (the most popular tool in the world)
- Multimodal: understands text, images, audio
- Good reasoning capability
- Constantly updated

**Ideal for**: General use, everyday tasks, office work

### Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic)

**The deep analyst.**

- Excellent with long documents (200K token window)
- Very strong at code analysis
- Better at tasks that require detailed reasoning
- More careful and nuanced responses
- Less prone to hallucinations in certain contexts

**Ideal for**: Document analysis, programming, technical writing

### Gemini 1.5 Pro (Google)

**The multimodal one par excellence.**

- Huge context window (up to 2 million tokens)
- Integrated with Google services
- Excellent with images and video
- Good with searches and up-to-date data
- Access to real-time information

**Ideal for**: Massive document analysis, using Google Workspace, searches

## The competitors

### Llama 3 (Meta)

**The open source champion.**

- Completely free and downloadable
- You can run it on your own computer
- No usage limits or API costs
- Active development community
- Excellent for experimentation

**Ideal for**: Developers, experimentation, offline use

### Mistral (Mistral AI)

**The efficient European.**

- French company (better GDPR compliance)
- Efficient and fast models
- Good quality/size ratio
- Competitively priced API

**Ideal for**: European companies, use with data restrictions

### DeepSeek

**The disruptive newcomer.**

- High-quality Chinese model
- Exceptional mathematical reasoning
- Very competitive price
- Rapid improvement in each version

**Ideal for**: Math tasks, logical reasoning, tight budgets

## Which one to choose?

### There is no "best" model

Each model has different strengths. The right question is not "which is the best?" but **"which is the best for MY use case?"**

### Recommended strategy

1. **For daily use**: GPT-4o or Claude (the most versatile)
2. **For long documents**: Claude 3.5 or Gemini 1.5
3. **For programming**: Claude 3.5 or GPT-4o
4. **For a tight budget**: Llama 3 or DeepSeek
5. **For privacy**: Llama 3 local or Mistral

<Callout type="tip">
Do not get married to a single model. Try several and find out which one works best for you. Preferences change over time as models improve.
</Callout>

## The landscape changes fast

This information may be partially outdated in a few months. Models constantly improve, new competitors appear and capabilities change.

The important thing is not to memorize who is the best today, but to understand **what to look for** when evaluating a model:

- How good is it for MY specific task?
- What is the cost per use?
- How fast does it respond?
- How well does it handle Spanish?
- How reliable is it?
