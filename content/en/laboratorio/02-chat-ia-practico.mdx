## Conversing with AI is an art

The laboratory's [AI Chat](/laboratorio) is much more than a search engine with answers. It is an assistant that can explain concepts, resolve doubts, generate examples and guide you in your learning. The quality of what you get depends directly on how you ask.

<Callout type="info">
Every interaction with the [chat](/laboratorio) is a learning opportunity. Do not just look for answers: look for understanding. Ask for explanations, examples and analogies.
</Callout>

## Prompting strategies

### Be specific

Instead of asking "What is a transformer?", try:

> "Explain what a transformer in AI is as if I were 12 years old. Include an analogy and a concrete example."

### Provide context

The AI gives better answers when it knows who you are and what you need the information for:

> "I am a marketing student. Explain to me how RAG works and how I could apply it to improve a customer service chatbot."

### Ask for specific formats

> "Give me 5 differences between GPT-6 Astra and Claude 5.1 in table format. Include a column with my recommendation according to the use case."

### Iteration

Do not expect the perfect answer on the first try. Converse with the AI:

1. First general question
2. Ask to go deeper into an aspect
3. Request an example
4. Ask for a simplification
5. Ask for it to be put into the context of your field

## Recommended uses

### To review concepts

Ask the AI to ask you questions about a topic. It is an excellent way to self-assess:

> "Act as an examiner. Ask me 5 questions about embeddings in AI. After each answer, tell me if it is correct and why."

### To generate examples

Concrete examples help to understand abstract concepts:

> "Give me 3 everyday examples of multimodal AI use that are not the typical ChatGPT or DALL-E ones."

### To connect topics

The AI can help you see relationships between concepts:

> "How are transformers, embeddings and attention related? Explain the connection between these three concepts."

<Callout type="tip">
Use the topic suggestions that appear in the [chat](/laboratorio) as a starting point. They are designed to cover the most important concepts and guide you in your exploration.
</Callout>

## Chat limitations

### What it does not do well

- **Very recent information:** it may not be up to date with the latest news
- **Exact calculations:** it is not a calculator, it can make mistakes with numbers
- **Subjective opinions:** it has no personal preferences
- **Predictions about the future:** any prediction is speculation

### What to do when the answer is not good

1. Rephrase the question with more context
2. Ask for a source or reference
3. Request a simpler or more detailed answer
4. Try another laboratory tool

## Privacy in the chat

Remember that conversations may be used to improve the system. Do not share sensitive personal information:

- **No:** "I am 30 years old, I live in Madrid and I work at San José school"
- **Yes:** "I am a secondary school teacher. Explain how to use AI in the classroom"
