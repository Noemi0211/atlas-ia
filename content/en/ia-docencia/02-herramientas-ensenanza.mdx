## Overview of AI educational tools

There is a growing ecosystem of tools designed specifically for the educational field. Some are complete platforms, others are specialised assistants. Knowing them is the first step towards integrating them judiciously.

<Callout type="info">
Not all tools work for all contexts. The key is to select those that align with your pedagogical objectives and your students' needs.
</Callout>

## General-purpose assistants

### ChatGPT and Claude

Ideal for generating adapted explanations, creating varied examples, drafting rubrics and preparing materials. Their strength lies in versatility: a single assistant can help you plan a teaching unit and draft an email to families.

### Perplexity

Excellent for teacher research. It searches for information with verifiable sources, useful for preparing up-to-date content on any curricular topic.

## Educational platforms with AI

### Khan Academy (Khanmigo)

Virtual tutor that guides students without giving the answer directly. It asks Socratic questions and offers progressive hints. It is one of the most advanced examples of AI tutoring.

### Quizlet

Generates flashcards, practice tests and explanations from any content. Its AI adapts the questions to each student's level.

### Duolingo

Although focused on languages, it is a benchmark in how AI can personalise learning, adjust difficulty and maintain motivation through gamification.

## Content creation tools

### Canva (Magic Studio)

Its AI features make it possible to generate presentations, infographics and visual materials in seconds. It is especially useful for creating attractive resources without requiring design skills.

### Gamma

Creates complete presentations from a prompt. Ideal for preparing visual materials quickly.

### Tome

Similar to Gamma, it allows generating visual narratives with AI, combining text and images coherently.

## Assessment tools

### Gradescope

Platform that uses AI to speed up the grading of exams and assignments, especially useful in large groups.

### Turnitin (with AI)

Its AI detector and feedback assistant help maintain academic integrity and offer consistent comments.

| Tool | Main function | Ideal for |

## Selection criteria

When choosing an AI tool for your classroom, consider:

1. **Privacy and data protection:** does it comply with regulations? What does it do with student data?
2. **Accessibility:** is it free or does it have a cost? Does it work offline?
3. **Language:** is it available in Spanish? Does it handle your language variety well?
4. **Learning curve:** how much time do you need to master it?
5. **Curricular integration:** does it align with your learning objectives?

<Callout type="warning">
Before using any tool with students, check its terms of use and the required minimum age. Many tools require users to be at least 13 or 16 years old.
</Callout>
