## AI as a content creator

One of the most immediate applications of AI in the classroom is the generation of educational content. From texts and exercises to images and presentations, AI can produce quality materials in a fraction of the time.

<Callout type="info">
AI does not replace teachers' creativity: it amplifies it. You bring the judgement, the experience and the human touch; AI brings speed and variety.
</Callout>

## Generating educational texts

### Adapted explanations

You can ask AI to explain a concept at different levels:

> "Explain what photosynthesis is to an 8-year-old. Use simple language, everyday examples and an analogy."

> "Explain the same concept to a high school student. Include scientific terminology and references to biochemical processes."

### Supplementary readings and texts

AI can generate short readings on any topic, with comprehension questions included:

> "Create a 300-word text about the importance of the oceans for the climate. Include 5 reading comprehension questions."

## Creating activities and exercises

### Types of activities

- **Multiple-choice questions:** with well-designed distractors
- **True/false exercises:** with justification
- **Case studies:** real situations to apply knowledge
- **Stepped problems:** from lower to higher difficulty
- **Role-playing games:** dialogues, debates, simulations

### Example prompt

> "Generate 10 multiple-choice questions about the solar system for 4th year of Primary education. Each question must have 4 options and the correct answer. Include a brief explanation of each answer."

<Callout type="tip">
Ask AI to generate the activities in table format so you can copy them directly into your materials. You will save formatting time.
</Callout>

## Multimedia content

### Images with AI

Tools such as DALL-E, Midjourney or Canva Magic Studio make it possible to generate educational images:

- Diagrams and visual schematics
- Illustrations for stories and readings
- Concept maps
- Decorative posters for the classroom

### Presentations

Gamma and Tome generate complete presentations from a text. You only need to indicate the topic and the tone:

> "Create an 8-slide presentation about the French Revolution for 2nd year of ESO. Include images, key ideas and a timeline."

## Ensuring content quality

### Critical review

All AI-generated content must go through your filter:

1. **Accuracy:** is the data correct?
2. **Suitability:** is the difficulty level appropriate?
3. **Clarity:** is the explanation understandable?
4. **Bias:** is there any implicit bias?
5. **Originality:** does it add value or is it generic?

<Callout type="warning">
Never use AI-generated content directly in the classroom without reviewing it first. AI can make mistakes, oversimplify or include outdated information.
</Callout>

## Efficient workflow

1. **Define** what you need (type of material, topic, level)
2. **Generate** with AI using detailed prompts
3. **Review** the content critically
4. **Adapt** it to your group's context
5. **Personalise** with local examples or your own experiences
6. **Publish** it in the format you usually use

| Exercises | ChatGPT / Claude | 10-15 min |
