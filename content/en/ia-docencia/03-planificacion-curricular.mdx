## AI as a co-planner

Curriculum planning is one of the tasks that consumes the most time for teachers. AI can act as a planning assistant that speeds up the process without replacing pedagogical judgement.

<Callout type="info">
AI does not design for you: it offers options, structures and suggestions that you evaluate, adapt and approve. The pedagogical judgement remains yours.
</Callout>

## Designing teaching units

### How to ask AI for help

To get good results when planning with AI, be specific in your instructions:

- **Context:** educational level, subject, duration of the unit
- **Objectives:** what students should learn
- **Approach:** active methodology, project, gamification, etc.
- **Available resources:** technology, materials, spaces

**Example of an effective prompt:**

> "You are an expert science teacher. Design a 6-session teaching unit on climate change for 3rd year of ESO (compulsory secondary education). Include objectives, activities, assessment criteria and a final project-based activity. Use an active methodology and foster critical thinking."

### Structure you can request

AI can generate for you:

- Teaching sequences session by session
- Starting, development and closing activities
- Guiding questions for each session
- Supplementary resources and readings
- Adaptations for diversity

## Generating objectives and assessment criteria

AI can help you draft learning objectives following taxonomies such as Bloom's:

> "Draft 5 learning objectives for a unit on fractions in 5th year of Primary education. Use Bloom's taxonomy verbs: remember, understand, apply, analyse, evaluate."

<Callout type="tip">
Ask AI to offer you objectives at different levels of difficulty. This way you can cater to the diversity of the classroom without extra effort.
</Callout>

## Timing and sequencing

AI can suggest realistic time distributions:

> "Distribute these contents across 8 sessions of 50 minutes. Indicate which activities to carry out in each session and what materials are needed."

### Advantages of assisted planning

| Aspect | Traditional planning | With AI |
|--------|----------------------|---------|
| Design time | 4-6 hours per unit | 30-60 minutes |
| Variety of activities | Limited by experience | Broad and diverse |
| Adaptation to diversity | Costly | Fast and simple |
| Content updating | Slow | Immediate |

## Curricular adaptations

One of the most powerful uses of AI is generating adaptations for students with specific needs:

> "Adapt this activity on the water cycle for a student with ADHD. Simplify the instructions, break the task into short steps and include visual elements."

AI can generate adapted versions of the same content in minutes, something that manually would require hours.

## Limitations and precautions

- **AI can hallucinate:** always verify factual data
- **It does not know your context:** review and adapt every suggestion
- **Bias:** AI can reflect cultural or gender biases
- **Dependency:** do not always plan with AI; alternate with your own design

<Callout type="warning">
Always critically review what AI generates. Ask yourself: does this make sense for my group? Does it align with the official curriculum? Is it pedagogically sound?
</Callout>
