## AI as an educational tool

Artificial intelligence is redefining the educational landscape. Far from replacing teachers, AI presents itself as an assistant capable of enhancing every stage of the educational process: planning, content creation, assessment and learning personalisation.

<Callout type="info">
AI in education does not seek to replace teachers, but rather to give them superpowers: automating repetitive tasks, personalising at scale and offering immediate feedback to every student.
</Callout>

## Areas of impact

### Learning personalisation

Each student has a different pace, style and level. AI makes it possible to adapt content, exercises and explanations to individual needs, something that is impossible to do manually in large classrooms.

### Automating administrative tasks

Grading exams, generating reports, communicating with families and organising schedules are tasks that AI can automate, freeing up teachers' time for what really matters: teaching.

### Analysis of educational data

AI can process large volumes of data on student performance to identify patterns, predict difficulties and suggest early interventions.

| Area | Without AI | With AI |
|------|------------|---------|
| Attention to students | Generic for the whole class | Personalised for each individual |
| Grading | Manual and slow | Automatic and immediate |
| Materials | Static | Dynamic and adaptable |
| Detection of difficulties | Reactive | Predictive |

## Integration models

There are three main models for integrating AI into the classroom:

- **Teacher assistant:** tools that help teachers prepare lessons, grade and generate materials
- **Intelligent tutor:** systems that interact directly with students to guide their learning
- **Learning analytics:** platforms that collect and analyse data to inform pedagogical decisions

## 21st-century digital skills for teachers

To make the most of AI in the classroom, teachers need to develop new competences:

1. **Basic understanding of AI:** what it is, how it works, what it can and cannot do
2. **Critical evaluation of tools:** selecting the right ones according to context and objectives
3. **Pedagogical design with AI:** integrating technology meaningfully into the teaching sequence
4. **Ethics and privacy:** protecting student data and using AI responsibly

<Callout type="tip">
You do not need to be a technology expert to use AI in your classroom. Many tools are designed for teachers without technical knowledge. Start with one simple tool and gradually explore more.
</Callout>
