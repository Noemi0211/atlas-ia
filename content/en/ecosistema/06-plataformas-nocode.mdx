## You do not need to code to use AI

No-Code AI platforms let you create automations, applications and complex workflows without writing a single line of code. They are perfect for entrepreneurs, marketing professionals and anyone who wants to take advantage of AI without being a programmer.

## The main platforms

### Make (ex-Integromat)

**Powerful visual automation.**

Make is like connecting Lego blocks, but for automations:

- **Visual interface**: Drag and drop modules
- **Thousands of integrations**: Connects with almost any application
- **Complex logic**: Conditionals, loops, filters
- **Built-in AI**: Use AI inside your automations
- **Templates**: Hundreds of predefined templates

**Ideal for**: Process automation, marketing, entrepreneurs.

**Price**: Free (1000 operations/month), from $9/month.

### Zapier

**The best known and easiest.**

Zapier is the most popular automation platform:

- **Easy to use**: Step-by-step setup
- **Simple Zaps**: Action → Reaction
- **Thousands of apps**: The largest number of integrations
- **AI with Copilot**: Helps create automations
- **Multi-step**: Complex workflows

**Ideal for**: Beginners, simple automations, personal use.

**Price**: Free limited, from $19.99/month.

## Common use cases

### Marketing and sales
- Automate emails when a lead fills in a form
- Publish to social media automatically
- Lead tracking
- Generate automatic reports

### Personal productivity
- Save important emails in Notion
- Create tasks in Asana from messages
- Sync contacts across platforms
- Summarise information from multiple sources

### E-commerce
- Get notified when there is a new order
- Update inventory automatically
- Send follow-up emails
- Generate sales reports

### Education
- Organise class materials
- Automate grading
- Send reminders to students
- Collect and organise research

## Practical example: automation with Make

Imagine you want to automate publishing a blog post:

1. **Trigger**: When you publish an article on your blog
2. **Action 1**: Make extracts the title and summary
3. **Action 2**: Generates a Twitter post with AI
4. **Action 3**: Creates an image for LinkedIn
5. **Action 4**: Publishes on both platforms

All of this happens automatically every time you publish.

## Make or Zapier?

### Choose Zapier if:
- You are a beginner in automation
- You need very specific integrations
- You want something simple and fast

### Choose Make if:
- You want more control and flexibility
- You need complex logic
- You want to save money (more operations for the same price)

### Choose n8n if:
- You know a bit about technology
- You want full control of your data
- You do not mind setting it up yourself

<Callout type="tip">
Start with Make. It has a generous free plan and its visual interface is more intuitive than Zapier for complex automations.
</Callout>

## Tips to get started

1. **Start simple**: One basic automation before the complex ones
2. **Use templates**: Do not reinvent the wheel, adapt existing templates
3. **Document**: Note down what each automation does
4. **Test**: Always test before activating
5. **Monitor**: Check that the automations are working correctly

<Callout type="warning">
Automations are powerful tools, but they do not replace strategic thinking. Use AI to execute, but you define the strategy.
</Callout>
