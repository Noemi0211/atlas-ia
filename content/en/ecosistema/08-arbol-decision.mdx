## Not sure what to choose? Let me help you

If you are overwhelmed by the options, this decision tree will guide you step by step. You only need to answer a few questions about what you need.

## How it works

1. **Read the first question**
2. **Select the option that best fits you**
3. **Follow the branch until you reach a recommendation**
4. **Explore the recommended tool**

<ArbolDecision />

## After the recommendation

Once you have a recommendation:

1. **Research more**: Read reviews and tutorials
2. **Try the free version**: Do not pay without trying
3. **Start small**: Do not try to use all the features at once
4. **Ask for help**: The community is your ally

## Important reminder

The decision tree is a guide, not an absolute truth. Your needs are unique and can change over time.

### What the tree does not consider:
- Your specific technical level
- Your exact budget
- Your personal interface preferences
- Compatibility with tools you already use

<Callout type="tip">
Use the tree as a starting point, but always check that the recommendation fits your particular situation.
</Callout>

## Final tips

1. **There is no perfect tool**: They all have pros and cons
2. **Start with one**: Master one before exploring others
3. **AI changes fast**: What is best today may be surpassed tomorrow
4. **You are the one who decides**: AI suggests, you decide
