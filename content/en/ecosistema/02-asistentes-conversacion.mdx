## Conversational assistants are the entry point

If you are starting out with AI, this is where you should begin. Conversational assistants are easy to use and versatile. You just type what you need and get an answer.

## The big three

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)

**The most popular in the world.**

ChatGPT became the fastest-growing application in history. And for good reason:

- **Versatility**: It works for almost everything (writing, analysing, coding, creating images)
- **Ease of use**: Simple and intuitive interface
- **Multimodal**: Understands text, images and audio
- **Plugins**: You can extend its capabilities
- **Memory**: Remembers previous conversations

**Strong point**: It is the "Swiss army knife" of AI. It is not the best at anything specific, but it is very good at everything.

**Weak point**: In very deep analyses, it can be less detailed than Claude.

### Claude (Anthropic)

**The deep analyst.**

Claude is known for its more careful and nuanced responses:

- **Huge context**: Up to 200K tokens (it can read very long documents)
- **Deep analysis**: It goes beyond the surface
- **Accuracy**: Less prone to hallucinations
- **Careful writing**: Well-structured responses
- **Code**: Excellent for programming

**Strong point**: When you need something analysed in depth, Claude is unbeatable.

**Weak point**: Fewer third-party integrations than ChatGPT.

### Gemini (Google)

**The multimodal one with massive context.**

Gemini has a unique advantage: its context can reach 2 million tokens:

- **Giant context**: It can process enormous documents
- **Google Workspace**: Integrated with Gmail, Docs, Sheets
- **Search**: Access to up-to-date information
- **Multimodal**: Excellent with images and video

**Strong point**: If you use Google, Gemini is the most natural option.

**Weak point**: Sometimes less accurate in detailed analyses than Claude.

## Which one to choose?

### For daily use
**ChatGPT**. It is the most balanced and the easiest to start using.

### For deep analysis
**Claude**. If you need to read long documents or do detailed analysis.

### For people who use Google
**Gemini**. Integration with the Google ecosystem is very practical.

### For a tight budget
All three have limited free plans. Try them out and keep the one that works best for you.

<Callout type="tip">
Do not commit to a single assistant. Many professionals use ChatGPT for quick tasks, Claude for deep analyses, and Gemini for context-based searches.
</Callout>

## Tips for using conversational assistants

1. **Be specific**: Do not ask "What is AI?", ask "Explain AI as if I were 10 years old"
2. **Provide context**: Tell it what you need the answer for
3. **Ask for format**: "Give it to me as a list", "Make a table", "Summarise it in 3 points"
4. **Iterate**: If the first answer is not perfect, ask it to improve it
5. **Verify**: Always check the important information
