## The AI that creates images is real

A few years ago, creating a professional image required artistic skills or hiring a designer. Now, with AI, you can generate impressive images by describing what you want.

## The three main options

### DALL-E 3 (OpenAI)

**The easiest to use.**

DALL-E is integrated directly into ChatGPT:

- **Easy**: Just describe what you want
- **Integrated**: Works inside ChatGPT
- **Good with prompts**: Understands natural descriptions
- **Editing**: Can modify existing images
- **Varied styles**: From photorealism to illustration

**Ideal for**: Beginners, quick illustrations, social media content.

**Price**: Included in ChatGPT Plus ($20/month).

### Midjourney

**The most artistic.**

Midjourney is known for its exceptional aesthetic quality:

- **Artistic quality**: Images with an impressive aesthetic
- **Styles**: Multiple artistic styles
- **Variations**: Can create variants of an image
- **Resolution upscaling**: Increases resolution while maintaining quality
- **Speed**: Generates images quickly

**Ideal for**: Digital art, visual concepts, professional graphic design.

**Price**: From $10/month.

### Stable Diffusion

**The open-source one.**

Stable Diffusion is free and can be run locally:

- **Free**: No usage costs
- **Local**: Runs on your computer
- **Customisable**: You can train your own models
- **Community**: Thousands of models and tools
- **Full control**: Adjust every parameter

**Ideal for**: Developers, technical artists, intensive use.

**Price**: Free (you need powerful hardware).

## How to write good prompts

The key to getting good images is **describing well what you want**:

### Basic structure
1. **Subject**: What appears in the image?
2. **Style**: What does it look like?
3. **Details**: What elements should be included?
4. **Quality**: What level of detail?

### Bad example
"A cat"

### Good example
"An orange cat sleeping on a red velvet armchair, warm sunset light, photorealistic style, 4K"

### Useful keywords
- **Style**: photorealistic, illustration, watercolour, pixel art, anime
- **Lighting**: natural light, studio light, sunset, neon
- **Composition**: close-up, wide shot, aerial view
- **Quality**: 4K, ultra detailed, masterpiece, best quality

<Callout type="tip">
Start with DALL-E 3 if you use ChatGPT. When you want more artistic quality, try Midjourney. If you want full control and do not mind the complexity, explore Stable Diffusion.
</Callout>

## Important limitations

1. **Text in images**: AI still struggles with visible text
2. **Hands and fingers**: It sometimes generates incorrect limbs
3. **Consistency**: It is difficult to maintain the same style across multiple images
4. **Copyright**: The legal landscape is still evolving

<Callout type="warning">
Use AI images responsibly. If you use them commercially, check the terms of service of each tool.
</Callout>
