## AI reaches audio and video

AI does not only work with text and images. Now it can create amazing voices, generate music and even edit complete videos. These tools are transforming content creation.

## Common use cases

### Podcasts and audiobooks
- Narration with professional voices
- Multiple characters with different voices
- Audio translation and localisation

### Social media content
- Short AI videos
- Avatars for presentations
- Generated background music

### Education
- Explainer videos
- Accessible materials (subtitles, audio)
- Content translation

### Marketing
- Advertising videos
- Presentations with avatars
- Custom music for campaigns

## Typical creation workflow

### For a podcast
1. Write the script
2. Generate the voice with ElevenLabs
3. Add background music with Soundraw
4. Edit with Audacity or Adobe Audition

### For a social media video
1. Write the script
2. Generate clips with Runway or Pika
3. Add voice with ElevenLabs
4. Edit and export with CapCut or Premiere

<Callout type="tip">
If you are starting out, ElevenLabs for audio and Runway for video are a powerful combination. Both have free plans to try them out.
</Callout>

## Ethics and considerations

1. **Deepfakes**: Do not use voice cloning to deceive
2. **Transparency**: Indicate when content is generated by AI
3. **Rights**: Check the terms of use for commercial content
4. **Quality**: AI improves fast, but it still has limitations

<Callout type="warning">
AI audio and video technology advances quickly. What is advanced today will be basic tomorrow. Stay up to date.
</Callout>
